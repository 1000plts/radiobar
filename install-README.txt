RadioBar — minimal menu bar internet radio
==========================================

INSTALL
1. Drag RadioBar.app into the Applications folder.
2. First launch only: RIGHT-CLICK RadioBar.app and choose "Open",
   then click "Open" in the dialog. (This is needed because the app
   isn't notarized by Apple — it's a friend-built app, not malware.)

USE
- RadioBar lives in the menu bar (no Dock icon).
- Right-click the menu bar item to pick a station.
- Left-click it to pause/resume.
- "Manage Stations…" lets you add, remove and drag-reorder stations.

NOTE
- Needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer).
